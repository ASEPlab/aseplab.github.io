# Advanced Separation Engineering for Clean Energy Systems and Critical Resource Recovery


**Mission: Advancing sustainable energy and critical resource recovery through innovative separation and purification technologies**

<img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/researchscope.png" alt="A detailed flowchart outlining the research scope of ASeP Lab, connecting target separation processes, critical mineral recovery, and renewable biomass valorization through integrated modeling and experimentation." style="width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: 0 auto;">

<strong style="display: block; text-align: center; font-size: 1.05em; margin-bottom: 8px; color: #222;">Figure Overview: Research Scope and Framework of the ASeP Lab</strong>
Our research framework tightly integrates multi-scale process modeling and hands-on experimentation to drive fundamental and applied innovations across three core directions: 
<strong>(1) Target Separation Processes & Vision</strong> (industry-relevant, robust, and scalable continuous separation technologies with minimized energy and chemical intensity), 
<strong>(2) Critical Mineral Recovery</strong> (advanced process engineering, cascade counter-current extraction, and continuous ligand-assisted ion exchange [CLIX]), and 
<strong>(3) Renewable Biomass Valorization</strong> (bio-derived extractants, zwitterionic adsorbents for downstream bioprocessing, and natural product purification).
</figcaption>
</figure>

Our research focuses on designing innovative separation processes through modeling and experimentation.

We utilize a comprehensive approach, striving to advance separation processes for recovering critical minerals and leveraging renewable resources.



## Research Interests

- Separation and Purification
- Adsorption / Chromatography / Extraction
- Critical Mineral Recovery
- Natural Product Separations
- Chiral Separations
- Downstream Biorocessing

## Open Source Software

* **ASeP LC Simulator**: A GUI tool for simulating various modes of liquid chromatography. Please email Dr. Choi if you have any questions. 
  

* **[CUP Modeler](https://github.com/NREL-SEPCON/CUPModeler)**: A free Python-based GUI tool for simulating various modes of liquid-liquid chromatography (counter-current chromatography and centrifugal partitioning chromatography) processes. Please email Dr. Choi if you have any questions. 
  

    * **Citation**: *Alherech M., Beckham G.T., & Choi H.* (2025). [Implementation and Evaluation of Multi-Dual Mode Counter-Current Chromatography in the CUP Modeler Software](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chroma.2024.466265). *Journal of Chromatography A*, 1760, 466265.

## Current Research Projects

* **Continuous Liquid-Liquid Chromatography Modeling and Simulations**
  * We are developing novel continuous liquid-liquid chromatography platforms optimized for critical resource recovery and challenging chemical separations.
  
* **Ligand Screening for Critical Mineral Separations**
  * We focus on high-selectivity ligand screening to enable efficient separation of critical minerals and apply these findings into chromatography separation platforms.
